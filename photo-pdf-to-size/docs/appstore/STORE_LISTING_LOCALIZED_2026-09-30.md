## en-US

Name: **Photo & PDF Resize: KB**
Subtitle: **Compress photos & PDFs**
Keywords: `compress,resize,image,photo,pdf,kb,mb,passport,visa,id,jpeg,pixels`

Promotional text:
Compress photos and PDFs to a target KB/MB limit, resize photos in pixels, and prepare technical photo files — locally on your iPhone.

Description:
Photo & PDF Resize is a focused utility for photos and PDF files that must meet a specific file-size limit.

BY FILE SIZE
Choose 100 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 5 MB, or enter your own maximum. The app finds suitable settings and verifies the final file size.

PDF BY FILE SIZE
Choose a PDF, set the maximum file size, and get a result at or below that limit. Processing stays on the iPhone. Strong compression may rasterize pages, so text search/selection can be lost.

BY DIMENSIONS
Set the long side to 600, 450, 300 px, or enter a custom value. Aspect ratio is preserved automatically.

DOCUMENTS
Prepare document-photo files with document-specific crop ratio, pixels, DPI, and file-size constraints. Current presets include Russia passport, U.S. 2×2 passport print, U.S. digital visa photo, India e-Visa, and UK passport print.

The app does not retouch faces, replace backgrounds, or promise government acceptance. It prepares transparent technical output for the selected preset.

BEFORE / AFTER
See the source and processed photo together with actual dimensions and file size before saving or sharing.

PRIVACY
Photo and PDF processing happens on the iPhone. No account is required. Internet access may be used to load ads; photo and PDF content is not uploaded for processing.

by Arvectum

## Screenshot order

1. Exact KB/MB result — real photo with visible Before / After, file size reduction.
2. Pixel resize result — same photo with Before / After and dimension change.
3. Documents — country/document selector and a concrete document preset.

Avoid claims such as “guaranteed accepted”, “officially compliant”, or “AI biometric approval”.
