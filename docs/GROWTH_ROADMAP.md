# Arvectum Tools — Growth Roadmap

**Owner:** distribution, monetization strategy, acquisition, retention and measurement across the portfolio.
**Exclusions:** app features, SDK integration, app-specific UX bugs, shared engineering platform.

## Discovery and positioning
- [ ] Maintain per-app search-intent and competitor research, linking to source reports in app docs.
- [ ] Track target markets, positioning and store localization opportunities based on evidence.

## ASO and SEO
- [ ] Maintain per-app App Store metadata/screenshot optimization experiments.
- [ ] Improve Arvectum website app catalog, landing pages, indexing and search attribution.
- [ ] Use one portfolio-level experiment register: hypothesis, target app, owner, metric, dates, result.

## Acquisition and retention
- [ ] Test organic channels, relevant communities, shareable demos and cross-promotion.
- [ ] Track listing-to-install conversion, cohorts/retention and ratings/reviews without intrusive UX.

## Monetization and analytics
- [ ] Maintain portfolio ad strategy and per-app revenue/review/retention experiments.
- [ ] Track installs, DAU/WAU, retention, ad impressions, fill, eCPM and revenue by app.
- [ ] Document SDK/UI implementation requests by linking to the owning app roadmap.

## Growth Agent
- [ ] Research and automate actionable ASO/SEO/market experiments with human approval for public posting.
