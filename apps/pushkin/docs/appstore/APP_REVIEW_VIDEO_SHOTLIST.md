# App Review physical-device recording

Apple specifically requested a recording of the submitted build on a physical device running the latest OS.

Required sequence:
1. Start from the iPhone Home Screen and launch PUSHKIN.
2. Show the initial History/Apps state.
3. Tap **Set up PUSHKIN**.
4. Show the Shortcuts handoff and complete/enable the automation.
5. Return to PUSHKIN.
6. Trigger one real notification from a configured app.
7. Show the notification in History.
8. Search for that notification.
9. Open the notification, then return.
10. Open Apps and add one supported app.
11. Briefly show Settings / Privacy policy.
12. End recording.

Do not show passwords, tokens, unrelated personal notifications, or developer/debug UI.
Use submitted build 1.0.0 (1), not the UI-rework branch.
