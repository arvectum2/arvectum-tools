# App Review physical-device recording — build 2

Apple requested a recording of the submitted build running on a physical iPhone.

Required sequence:
1. Start from the iPhone Home Screen and launch PUSHKIN.
2. Show the initial History state.
3. Tap Set up PUSHKIN.
4. Show the Shortcuts handoff and complete/enable the automation.
5. Return to PUSHKIN.
6. Trigger one real notification from a configured app.
7. Show the notification in History.
8. Search for that notification and open it briefly.
9. Return to History and tap +; show the Add App picker.
10. Open Settings; show capture status, Add app, local storage, Privacy and Support.
11. End recording.

Do not show passwords, tokens, unrelated personal notifications, or developer/debug UI.
Use the exact submitted binary: 1.0.0 (2).
